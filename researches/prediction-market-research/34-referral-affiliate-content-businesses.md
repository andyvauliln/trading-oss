# 34 - Referral, affiliate, creator and content businesses
_Band: established-emerging · Evidence grade: B (A for the programme terms and the own on-chain census of every referral payout since March 2026; B for the CJR, WSJ-via-TechCrunch and security-vendor reports; C for CreatorDB and affiliate-directory figures; D for newsletter, course and signal-group revenue, none of which is disclosed) · Last verified: 2026-09-29_

### Referral, affiliate, creator and content businesses
- **One-line summary**: Earn from other people's trading instead of your own: the venue's referral programmes (10% / 5% of net prediction fees for 30 days, 20% of Perps fees with no stated end date, $25 US credits), privately negotiated creator and affiliate deals, and newsletters or education that funnel readers into those links. The prediction referral stream paid $11.3M to 9,448 wallets from March to September 2026 ($10.9M of it by 7 July), then fell 96% overnight and now pays about $3.9k a day in total. The fringe of this business (fake-PnL videos, "AI bot" posts that lead to wallet drainers, malware-laced bot repos) is fraud.
- **Category**: Service & data business [audience and distribution only; software and data products are file 33, fee-on-flow apps are file 32]
- **Maturity**: Established as a practice, decayed as an income for newcomers. Evidence by sub-variant: (1) official prediction referral: hard data. An own census of every transfer from the two referral distributors (two plain wallets that DefiLlama labels as such [23]; Polymarket's own `referral_income` field equals their on-chain totals to the cent for the three largest earners [24]) shows $11.33M paid to 9,448 wallets from 25 March to 29 September 2026 (PolyScalping's independent count was $11.3M to 9,329 wallets on 20 September [E2]). Daily payouts ran $64-195k from April to the 7 July run, $2.6-9.8k from 8 July to August, and $2.1-6.6k a day in September. (2) Perps referral: terms are public [2], payouts are internal-ledger only and not observable; the pool is bounded by $685k of Perps fees in the last 30 days [4]. (3) Polymarket US refer-a-friend: $25 of non-withdrawable credit each, 50 referrals maximum [7]; the US affiliate programme is application-only with private terms [8]. (4) Paid creator and news-account deals: real and large but undisclosed in price; at least 11 Polymarket-badged X accounts [13], 88 sponsored YouTube creators tracked for Polymarket and 364 for Kalshi [12][11], and a WSJ-reported scandal over paid fake-win videos [14]. (5) Newsletters, education, paid signal groups: no revenue or paid-subscriber figure exists for any Polymarket-focused product (E6 found none [E6]; none found here). (6) Scam fringe: established criminal practice, documented by four security vendors in 2026 [16][17][18][19]. The brief's product sub-variants (analytics SaaS, datasets, APIs, feeds, calibrated forecasts, no-code builders, bot development, vaults and OTC) are covered in file 33 and file 84, where the critic's "empty shell" finding is addressed.
- **Requires AI?**: optional. An LLM can draft posts from your own bot logs and summarise market moves. It adds no edge, and "AI bot made $X" content is the scam lure described below.
- **How it works (simple flow)**:
  1. Qualify. The prediction referral pays only after you have $10,000 of lifetime trading volume [1]. The Perps referral has no volume bar but starts with 10 invites, rising to 25, then 100 / 250 / 500 at $100k / $500k / $1M of combined Perps volume [2]. The Polymarket US credit needs a US account and a funded friend [7].
  2. Build a channel with a reason to exist: numbers-first posts from your own bots' logs, a public dataset or leaderboard, or market commentary. Attribution: a prediction referral counts if the new user signs up within 30 days of clicking your market or profile link [1]; a Perps code binds once and cannot be changed or self-applied [2].
  3. Earn. Prediction: 10% of the referee's *net* fees (after the referee's own taker-rebate tier) and 5% on second-level referees. It stops at 30 days after sign-up or when the referee reaches Platinum, whichever comes first. It is paid daily in pUSD; fee-free markets (geopolitics, NFL game days) pay nothing [1][6]. Perps: 20% of the referee's fees, weekly, no per-referee cap and no stated end date [2]. US: a one-off $25 credit per funded friend [7].
  4. Add private deals: sponsorships, "promo code" affiliate pages, or badge-and-payment arrangements with the venue. These deals are negotiated by email (affiliate@polymarket.com), and neither venue publishes rates [8][10].
  5. Reconcile: payouts arrive as pUSD transfers from `0x8a80356b…24ee` in the ~00:35 UTC run [22]. `/v2/user-stats` has a `referral_income` field, but it has added nothing since the 11 September run (checked on five of the ten largest September earners) [24].
  6. Stay honest: disclose every link and sponsorship in the content itself, never show atypical wins without the typical outcome, and never trade against what you publish [20][E9].
- **What creates the edge**: Audience ownership and nothing else. The venue pays for distribution because a new trader loses a few dollars to fees and spread every month: in Pew's sample of 11,989 active wallets the average trader spent a little over $600 in six weeks and ended with a net loss of less than $2 [21]. Nobody is "on the other side" of this trade in the market sense. You are paid out of the venue's acquisition budget, and the venue can cut it without notice, as it did. The rate fell from 30% / 10% of gross fees in March [33][E2] to 10% / 5% of net fees for 30 days from 28 May [1]. Payouts did not follow on that date: the daily series rose from ~$95k in late May to $120-187k in the 26 June-7 July runs (World Cup season). It fell to $7.5k on the 8 July run, with recipients down from 2,040 to 578, and has not recovered [22]. The changelog is silent on 8 July. One reading is that referrals attributed before 28 May kept the old terms until then; this is unverified.
- **Capital required**: <$1k. Content costs time. The only capital requirement is the $10k of lifetime trading volume behind the prediction referral, which costs $0-$350 in fees depending on category (derived from [6]; whether fee-free volume counts is unverified). Paid acquisition is possible but does not pay back (see Results by investment size). There is no lock-up: payouts are daily (prediction) or weekly (Perps).
- **Technical requirements**: A publishing stack (Substack or a static site), your own bot logs turned into charts, referral links on every market mention, and a payout tracker. The tracker is one `eth_getLogs` filter on pUSD transfers from the distributor to your wallet, because the Data API field is stale [22][24]. Add a disclosure checklist per post (FTC guides [20]; jurisdiction list: the international venue is off-limits to US persons and the UK is close-only [E9]). Build time is 1-2 weeks solo. Building an audience that earns more than the median referrer takes months, with no public data on how long (guess: 6-18 months).
- **Opportunity size**: Small and shrinking for referrals; unknown and private for creator deals.
  - Prediction referral, whole venue: $116,159 in the last 30 runs (31 Aug-29 Sep) to 1,146 wallets, about $3.9k a day. That is ~2% of the $174k a day paid as taker rebates (file 28) [22].
  - Before the collapse, the 30 runs to 7 July paid $4.46M to 4,932 wallets, 79 of them receiving $10k or more [22].
  - Perps referral: the ceiling is 20% of $685,358 in 30-day fees, i.e. $137k a month if every Perps trader had a referrer. Perps volume was $2.91B in those 30 days, with fees averaging 2.4 bps because beta accounts sit on the top tier [3][4][5].
  - US credits: at most 50 × $25 = $1,250 of non-withdrawable credit per participant [7].
  - Creator budgets: undisclosed. CreatorDB counts 505 sponsored Polymarket YouTube posts (28.2M views) against 4.4K for Kalshi (223.2M views), which suggests Kalshi is the bigger paying sponsor today (vendor data) [11][12].
- **Pros**: Revenue uncorrelated with your trading PnL. No market risk. Reuses assets you already have: bot logs, wallet screens and anti-Sybil tooling, which make better content than affiliate SEO. Referral links sit on top of builder-fee routing (file 32) and data products (file 33) at no extra cost. The Perps referral is the only programme with a lasting tail.
- **Cons / risks**:
  - Programme risk: all terms "can change at any time without notice" [1][2], and they did, by 96% in one day [22].
  - The prediction referral is an acquisition rebate, not an annuity: 30 days, capped at Platinum, and zero on fee-free categories [1].
  - Concentration and competition: the venue's own free newsletter has over 91,000 subscribers [26]; badged accounts get more than 3x the official account's impressions [13]; sports media run the US promo-code pages [34].
  - Reputational contagion from the fake-win scandal [14] and the drainer ecosystem [15].
  - Legal: undisclosed promotion (FTC), atypical-results claims (FTC), signal selling (CTA question), talking your book (CFTC §6(c)(1), Santos) [20][30][32].
  - Operational: every "bot" repo you study is a supply-chain risk to your own keys [16][17][18][19].
- **Expected results**: Hard data on the current regime (last 30 runs) [22]:
  - The median recipient got $19.94; 247 recipients got at least $100 and 20 got at least $1,000; nobody got $10k.
  - The top wallet got $3,452 (3.0% of the pool) and the top ten 15.9%.
  - Over the last 7 runs, $21,322 went to 477 wallets, and nobody got $1,000.
  - Whole history since March: 15 wallets earned more than $100k, and the top wallet $667,214 (5.9%) [22][24].
  - Who they are: of the top 50 all-time earners, 18 have under $100k of their own lifetime USDC volume and took $1.89M, so they are distribution accounts. 19 are $1M+ traders who took $2.56M. One account created on 3 June received $395,819 in 39 payout days with $25k of its own volume. Three accounts with 5-16 lifetime trades received $139k-$187k each [24][25]. Public data cannot say which were communities, apps or referral farms.
  - Derived unit economics: an average active wallet (~$420 spent in 30 days, pro-rated from Pew's six-week average, at 1.5-2.5% effective fees [21][6]) is worth about $0.65-$1.05 to you, once. A $10k-a-month sports bettor is worth ~$23 in their first month. A $1M-a-month Perps taker is worth ~$47-$80 a month for as long as they trade [2][3][4].
  - Content with honest numbers is rare and does not sell by itself: kacho.io published a full bot PnL (+$4,973.06 on $95,830 over 3,858 bets, monthly profit decaying from $2,506 to $180) with only an email capture [28]. Show HN posts naming Polymarket drew a median of 1.5 points (24 launches, maximum 5) [29].
  - Guess for a solo builder without an audience: $0-$60 a month. With ~10k engaged readers: $100-$1,000 a month from referrals, plus sponsorships at unknown rates.
- **Competitive landscape**: Crowded at the top, empty in the middle. Incumbents:
  - the venue itself (The Oracle, over 91,000 subscribers, "always free" [26]);
  - badged news accounts such as @NewsWire_US (200M+ impressions a month) and dozens of paid partners including Piers Morgan and Laura Loomer [13];
  - sports-betting affiliates running "$50 bonus" pages (Covers is a named Polymarket US partner [34]);
  - Kalshi's 364 sponsored YouTube creators [11].
  Independent prediction-market newsletters are small: Forecasting Newsletter over 2,000, Stacy in Dataland over 3,000 subscribers [26]. What it takes: a distinctive, verifiable dataset or track record, since fake PnL is the category default (file 21: five viral bot claims checked against primary data, none survived; file 26).
- **Regulatory/ethical flag**: Two tiers.
  - **Grey at most** for a disclosed referral or affiliate core. The FTC Endorsement Guides (revised 2023) require the affiliate relationship to be disclosed in the content itself, not only next to the link. A platform's paid-partnership tag may not be enough. "Results not typical" does not cure an atypical-win claim: you must show the generally expected outcome [20]. For Pew's population that is roughly break-even, with 7% up and 9% down more than $1,000 [21].
    - US promotion depends on the outcome of state legal fights over prediction markets (matters in 28 states [E9]).
    - The international venue bars US persons and residents of ~30 other jurisdictions; marketing it to them invites ToS breaches (and, in the US, state-law exposure: inference) [E9].
    - Paid signal groups with a US nexus raise the CTA question: CFTC Letter 26-25 excludes "express 'buy' or 'sell' signals" from its relief [30][31].
    - A creator who holds positions and posts misleading claims is in Santos territory (CEA §6(c)(1)) [32].
  - **Prohibited:**
    - self-referral, referring accounts you control, and "inauthentic trading" (clawback [1]; Sybil detection in file 29);
    - self-referral and coordinated activity on the US venue [7];
    - undisclosed paid promotion and staged wins (the WSJ case [14]);
    - custodial drainer bots and malware repos, which are fraud and computer crime [15][16][17][18][19].
  - This file stays at survey level for these: what was done, how it was found, what followed.
- **How you'd validate it**: A 4-week pilot at ~$0 plus ~20 hours. Publish four posts built from your own suite's logs, each with a disclosed prediction referral link and a Perps code. Track sign-ups on the referral dashboard and income from the on-chain distributor transfers to your wallet (not `/v2/user-stats`, which is stale [24]). Pass: at least $100 of referral income in the 30 days after the last post, or at least 500 engaged subscribers. Otherwise treat referral links as free garnish on files 32 and 33 and stop investing time. Separately, email the US affiliate address and Kalshi for terms before building anything around CPA [8][10].

### Speed and execution sensitivity
- **Speed class**: speed-insensitive. Income depends on audience and calendar timing (days), never on latency.

| Part of the strategy | What it depends on | If you are slower or execute worse | Realistic target for a solo team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Content production | own logs, charts, a verifiable claim | commentary without data is indistinguishable from affiliate SEO | one data-backed post a week |
| Timing | the 30-day referral window and the event calendar | referees who join after the NFL / election peak earn you little | publish 1-3 weeks before fee-paying peaks; not before fee-free events [1][6] |
| Attribution | 30-day click window; Perps code binds once [1][2] | lost referees, no remedy | links on every market mention; code in bio |
| Payout reconciliation | distributor transfers at ~00:35 UTC [22] | stale API field hides under-payment [24] | daily `eth_getLogs` job |
| Compliance review | FTC, state, CTA, venue ToS [20][E9] | clawback, takedown, liability | checklist per post |

- **How speed changes the result**: (1) The 30-day cap makes timing the only lever. A heavy referee who joins during a fee-paying peak pays most of their first-month fees inside your window; one who joins before a fee-free geopolitics run pays you nothing [1][6]. (2) Heavy referees exit early at Platinum (32% tier, $1M of 30-day weighted volume), so the best referees are the shortest-lived [1][file 28]. (3) Latency, rate limits and uptime are irrelevant.
- **Where to run it**: anywhere. A $0-$10 VPS or a laptop cron for the payout tracker. Do not pay for co-location or data feeds.

### Results by investment size
Assumptions: capital buys acquisition and runway, not positions. Revenue = prediction referral (10% of net fees, 30 days) + Perps referral (20%, recurring) + sponsorship; no audience at the start; hosting $0-$20 a month; the $10k lifetime-volume bar is met from your own trading. Every figure is labelled.

| Capital | Workable? | How it is deployed | Gross / month | Running costs / month | Net / month | Net / capital / month | Binding constraint |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $1k | yes, as a side channel | own time; newsletter and posts from bot logs; disclosed links; no ads | $0-$60 (guess; the current median referrer earns $20 [22]) | $0-$20 | -$20 to +$60 | -2% to +6% | audience |
| $5k | no, if the money buys ads | ~$800 a month of paid promotion over 6 months | $30-$300 (derived: ~$1 per average referee, a few heavy ones [21][22]) | $800-$850 incl. ads | -$820 to -$500 | -16% to -10% | acquisition cost vs ~$1 of referral value per average referee |
| $10k | only with an existing audience (~10k engaged readers) | referral + Perps code + 1-2 disclosed sponsorships; money funds a dataset and design | $150-$3,500 (referral part $20-$1,000: above $1k you would be top-20 venue-wide, the top September referrer got $3,452 [22]; the rest is sponsorship at private rates, guess) | $50-$150 | +$0 to +$3,350 (guess) | 0% to +33% | audience size and trust, not capital |

- **Minimum sensible capital**: ~$0 plus the $10k of lifetime trading volume the prediction referral requires [1].
- **Capacity ceiling**: The venue-wide pools, not your capital: ~$116k a month of prediction referral paid to everyone [22] and at most ~$137k a month of Perps referral [4]. Beyond that you depend on private deals.
