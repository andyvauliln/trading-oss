# 27 - Whale / informed-flow tracking, fast-follow and follower-flow trades
_Band: established-emerging · Evidence grade: B (A for the on-chain screens, builder-code fill labels, fee types and book depth from papers and own API queries; C for every follower result, all anecdotal or self-reported; D for alert-product claims) · Last verified: 2026-09-28_

### Whale / informed-flow tracking, fast-follow and follower-flow trades
- **One-line summary**: Read the public tape for the fingerprint of informed money (fresh wallet, one market family, conviction size at a low price, common funder) and react within seconds: follow it, pull or skew your quotes against it, or sell the alert. Detection is measurable and academically validated; profitable following is not documented anywhere, and the strongest use is defensive.
- **Category**: Information edge [flow; a detector shared by the maker bots of files 02, 04, 05 and the copy bot of file 26]
- **Maturity**: Emerging for the buildable half, and split from the illegal half on purpose (file 30 covers insiders themselves, which is Established practice). What is proven: the screens work after the fact. A lifecycle heuristic from Gomez-Cram, Guo, Jensen and Kung (SSRN working paper, April 2026), reported in [1] (account created within seven days before an event, trades only that event, at least $1,000 of volume and $1,000 of realised profit, no trades after resolution), flags 1,950 accounts with a mean realised profit of $15,012.92 (median $2,758.29), and their order imbalance predicts next-period price changes (t = 2.54) and final outcomes (t = 8.65) [1]; a five-signal screen over 210,718 wallet-market pairs finds a 69.9% win rate and ~$143M of anomalous profit (Feb 2024 - Feb 2026) [2]; ACDC's "Orca" rule (>= $2,500 within an hour at odds <= 35%) yields 556 wallets, 152 of them military-focused with a 97.2% win rate and ~$8M of profit through 2026-05-05 [3]; the Google engineer's wallet went 22/23 on Year-in-Search markets from 3c entries while losing on 69 other markets, so the signal only exists once PnL is cut by market family [4]. What is not proven: that a follower earns. Neither the SSRN paper (known here only through [1] and E4's press coverage) nor [1] tests following; the one practitioner who tried "did not succeed in my detection algorithm" [5]; the copycat evidence is two wagers of $200,000 and $100,000 behind one Orca before the June 2025 Iran strikes, with no PnL [3]; every alert product sells screens, not results [11][12][13]. Own check today: of the 544 wallets with a >= $2,000 taker fill in the last 8.9 hours, 12 met the Orca rule outside sports and crypto, and none of them was younger than 32 days; the five wallets younger than a week were all sports or crypto bettors (one two-hour-old wallet bought $94.7k of an NFL total at 0.52) [18]. The detector fires on rich gamblers most days and on insiders a few times a year.
- **Requires AI?**: optional. The detectors are arithmetic on the tape (age, concentration, size, price, funder graph). An LLM helps offline to map markets to "families" (the Google case was invisible until the wallet's PnL was segmented by family [4]) and to tag which markets a small set of people could know in advance (military operations, corporate announcements, speech scripts); it has no role at event time.
- **How it works (simple flow)**:
  1. Ingest fills in real time from three public sources: the Data API `/trades` stream (wallet, name, market, side, size, price, hash; `takerOnly=true` by default, add `filterType=CASH&filterAmount=2000` to see only large fills) [18]; the `OrderFilled` events of the CTF Exchange V2 contract, which carry maker, taker and the `builder` field [16][22]; and `GET clob.polymarket.com/builder/trades?builder_code=...`, unauthenticated, 300 fills per page, with `tradeType` (TAKER / MAKER), `sizeUsdc`, `builderFee`, `owner` and `maker` per fill [17].
  2. Maintain per-wallet lifecycle features: first-activity time (`/activity?user=&sortDirection=ASC&limit=1`), number of markets ever traded, share of notional in one family, largest fill, funding source of the proxy wallet (one funder feeding many proxies is the Sybil signature of file 29) [18][1].
  3. Score each large fill: Orca rule (>= $2,500 in an hour at <= 35%) [3]; lifecycle rule (age < 7 days, one event, >= $1,000) [1]; family-restricted win rate for older wallets [4]; category prior (military long-shots succeed 52% of the time versus 25% in politics and 14% overall, FT / ACDC) [15].
  4. React by mode. Fast-follow: buy the same side at the touch within seconds, size-capped by the resting depth, hold to the information event. Quote-pull: cancel or widen your maker quotes in that market and its siblings for minutes to hours (files 02, 04, 05). Alert: push the scored fill to Telegram or a dashboard, which is what PolyTrack, Presage and Polysights sell [11][12][13].
  5. Label flow by app. Any builder's fills are enumerable: MetaMask's last 300 fills were 100% taker, median $4.43, paying a 400 bps builder fee; PolyCop 90% taker, median $1.53, 50 bps; Kreo 96% taker, median $1.50, 0 bps; betmoar 22% taker, median $11.27, 0 bps (own query, 2026-09-28) [17]. Wallet-app flow is uninformed retail; copy-bot flow is followers. A maker leans into the former and treats the latter as a cascade indicator.
  6. Evaluate every alert on the resolved outcome, by category, and retire rules whose precision is below the fee-adjusted break-even (see validation).
- **What creates the edge**: The ledger is public and pseudonymous, so information that a securities market would hide (who traded, how large, from how new an account) is visible to everyone within seconds. The other side is whoever leaves resting orders in a market where a small set of people know the answer: retail limit orders and makers without a toxicity filter. Insiders keep losing information to the tape because size at a low price in a fresh wallet is the only way to monetise a one-off secret; their trades move price 7-12x more per dollar than skilled trades (LBS / Yale via [E4]), and 3.14% of accounts are the "skilled winners" who drive most price discovery (Gomez-Cram et al. via [1] and [E4]). Why followers rarely capture it: the first mover sweeps the book, so the follower buys at the post-sweep price; the depth left behind is small (touch sizes of $1-$2k on most of today's top geopolitics markets, $32k on one) [20]; leaders know they are watched and run secondary accounts, iceberg under alert thresholds and exit by merging YES and NO so no sale prints ("You can become exit liquidity for them"), Polymarket's own newsletter says [7]; and most large fills are noise: sports was 1,182 of today's 1,500 large taker fills, geopolitics 46 and politics 49 [18]. The timing evidence cuts both ways: on the $269M "US forces enter Iran by April 30" market the 30-minute and 2-hour pre-event leakage scores were exactly zero [9], and only 88 of 12,708 candidate markets (0.7%) yield a computable leakage score at all [10]. Detection is a rare-event problem with a high false-positive rate, which is why its value is highest as a quote-pull trigger (avoiding one adverse fill is worth the same as capturing it) and lowest as a standalone taker strategy.
- **Capital required**: <$1k for the detector and the quote-pull mode (it protects capital deployed elsewhere); $1k-$10k for fast-follow, because clips are depth-capped at hundreds to low thousands of dollars per alert and capital is locked until the information event (hours in military markets, weeks in corporate and election markets). Fees: geopolitics is fee-free (372 of the 437 live markets in the 100 most active geopolitics events carry no `feeType`; 765 of 983 across 300 events) [20][E1]; politics 0.04 x p(1-p), economics 0.05 x p(1-p) [E1]. Alert products need no trading capital.
- **Technical requirements**: (1) Data in: Data API `/trades` polling or an `OrderFilled` indexer (Goldsky pipelines, Envio HyperSync, or polyledger-style DuckDB from an RPC) [22][23][E6]; wallet feature store; funder graph from Polygon transfers (public RPCs prune logs within a day, see file 14). (2) Scoring: the three rules above plus a market-family map. (3) Reaction: for quote-pull, a hook into the maker bot's cancel path (batch cancel, 40 orders/s standard tier [E1]); for fast-follow, FAK orders capped at touch depth; for alerts, a Telegram bot. (4) Risk: per-alert notional cap, per-day loss stop, a rule that never follows into a market you cannot name the information source for. (5) Test: a replay harness over resolved markets with cut-off staggering (features computed only from fills before the alert). Build: 2-4 weeks solo for detector plus quote-pull on an existing maker stack; the family map and funder graph are the open-ended part.
- **Opportunity size**: Bounded by what informed money leaves behind, not by its profits. The measured informed profit pool is ~$143M over two years [2], ~$8M for military Orcas over roughly the same window [3]; the follower's share is the price move after the alert times the depth available. Today's tape: 1,500 taker fills >= $2,000 in 8.9 hours (median $3,871, p90 $12,540, largest $342,878), $8.8M in sports, $0.29M in geopolitics, $0.25M in politics, $0.12M in economics [18]. After the twelve largest flagged non-sports fills today the price 30 minutes later was within +3.5c of the fill price in every case (Fed decision markets +3c to +3.5c, Israel PM markets +0.5c then reverting, Senate races +0.5c), against a 1c quoted spread [19]; a follower who paid the spread captured at most 2-3c on a few thousand dollars of depth. Direction: shrinking for followers (enforcement: DOJ and CFTC cases in 2026, Israeli indictments, Polymarket's referral of "dozens of trader wallets", the April 2026 builder audit over "find insiders before everyone else" marketing) [3][6][15]; stable or growing for detection, since every new fee-free geopolitics ladder is where the largest cases sit [E4].
- **Pros**: Free data with no rate-limit pain at alert volumes; the screens are published, replicable and validated on prosecuted cases to within $1.03 of the DOJ figure [1][4]; the same detector defends every maker and copy bot the owner already runs; the alert product needs no capital and has visible demand (PolyTrack at $10/week or $99.99 lifetime, Presage's waitlist, Polysights' Insider Finder) [11][12][13]; builder-code labelling of retail versus follower flow is free and no directory tool advertises it [17][E6].
- **Cons / risks**: Precision: most flagged wallets are gamblers (today's fresh five were all sports and crypto) [18]; leakage is computable in 0.7% of markets [10]. Execution: you are last in a queue that includes bots with six-figure size [3]; depth after the sweep is thin [20]; the leader may be baiting followers or exiting into them [7]. Legal: knowingly trading alongside suspected classified-information leaks is unexamined ground; trading ahead of your own users or followers is front-running under ToS §4.2 and Polymarket US Rule 7.2(j); an alert product marketed as "trade with insiders" triggered a platform audit [6][E9]; use of Polymarket Data by a proprietary trading firm "including on-chain" needs a written agreement [E9]. Capital: locked until the event; a wrong follow into a 3c-to-97c market is a total loss. Edge decay: alert products are commoditising the rules, so the follower queue lengthens while insider frequency falls with enforcement.
- **Expected results**: no public data for followers; the only numbers are anecdotal: a copycat bot and a whale placed $200,000 and $100,000 after one Orca in June 2025 [3]; a Stand user "makes over $10k per month" (self-reported, method unstated) [8]; the practitioner who built the detector "did not succeed" and, on following, found that "just following up on these types of trades didn't provide a very big edge, as the nature of the trade adjusts the payout percentage" [5]. Insider-side numbers are not follower numbers: mean $15,012.92 per flagged account [1], ~$485,000 from $38,500 on Maduro, ~$553,000 on the Iran strike, > $1M on Year in Search [2]. Guess for a solo fast-follower in fee-free geopolitics and politics: -3% to +4% a month on the following capital before fixed costs, with fat tails, dominated by a few true positives a year and dozens of false ones; the quote-pull mode is worth what it avoids: passive longshot fills in E4's sample show +17% at one hour and -36% at resolution [E4], so pulling one toxic fill a week on a $10k maker book is plausibly worth $100-$500 a month (derived guess).
- **Competitive landscape**: Crowded at the alert layer (PolyTrack, Presage, Polysights, PolyInsider, Stand's whale feed by shrimp / dolphin / whale brackets [8][11][12][13][E6]), academic tooling public (ForesightFlow ILS papers) [1][9][10], and Reddit detectors that flagged the Venezuela wallets "hours before headlines" [14]. Followers include bots that fire within seconds ("unrecognized wallet addresses make major buys on the whale feed before the well known traders, usually a few seconds from one another"); Stand's example was tariff markets "when the US-China agreement news broke", so the fastest wallets on a whale feed are public-news takers (file 17), not insiders, and a fast-follower queues behind them [8]. Own check: copy-bot cascades are weak today: only 2 of 45 PolyCop taker fills and 1 of 45 Kreo fills followed a >= $500 same-side fill in the same market within 120 s (lags 20-115 s), and copy-bot builder volume fell 77% from April to August 2026 [17][E6]. Being competitive means precision, not speed: a family map and funder graph nobody sells, and a maker book to protect.
- **Regulatory/ethical flag**: grey. Copying public on-chain trades is allowed on both venues [E9]. Prohibited: trading ahead of your own followers, users or signal subscribers (ToS §4.2 front-running; Polymarket US 7.2(j) "nonpublic information regarding an impending transaction by another Person"), baiting followers with misleading orders or posts (Santos, CFTC 2026-07-31), and acting in concert to move a price [E9]. Selling "insider" alerts is legal but is now the feature Polymarket audits builders over [6]; PolyTrack's own disclaimer says "most flagged trades are not insider trading" [11]. Knowingly following flow you believe to be classified-information leakage has no case law; the CFTC's 2026-02-25 advisory reaches insiders through Reg 180.1, not followers [E9].
- **How you'd validate it**: Replay, two weeks, no money. Build the three screens over the public trade archive (Hugging Face Polymarket-v1 to 2026-04-28, then the Data API) [E6]; for every alert record the price 1 min, 30 min and 24 h later and at resolution, the depth at the touch, and the category. Metrics: precision by rule and category (share of alerts whose side won), and follower PnL at the post-alert touch net of fee and spread. Pass for fast-follow: >= 300 alerts, positive net PnL in geopolitics and corporate with a 95% bootstrap interval above zero; fail if, as [5] found, the signal is absorbed within the alert latency. Pass for quote-pull: on the maker bot's own fill log, the flagged fills' resolution PnL is worse than unflagged by more than the rebate forgone. Then a $500 forward pilot with FAK orders capped at touch depth. Cost: ~30 engineering hours, free data, a small VPS.

### Speed and execution sensitivity
- **Speed class**: latency-sensitive. The Data API tape and an RPC log subscription put a fill in front of you 1-3 s after the match; bots on the whale feed act "a few seconds from one another" [8], so a follower needs seconds, not milliseconds, and the quote-pull needs to beat the next informed clip rather than the first.

| Part of the strategy | What it depends on | If you are slower or execute worse | Realistic target for a solo team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fill detection | Data API polling vs `OrderFilled` subscription | you see the fill after the bots have swept the touch | 1-3 s from match via RPC logs; 2-5 s via `/trades` polling |
| Wallet features | cached age, concentration, funder | a cold lookup (`/activity` ASC) costs 200-400 ms per wallet [18] | pre-computed for every wallet seen in 30 days |
| Fast-follow entry | FAK at the touch, depth-capped | paying 1-3c more than the leader; partial fills | order out within 1 s of the alert; expect $500-$3k clips [20] |
| Quote-pull | batch cancel on the maker book | one more toxic fill on the wrong side | cancel within 1 s; 40 orders/s standard tier [E1] |
| Exit | information event or repriced book | holding through a reversal, leader exiting into you [7] | event-driven, never chase |

- **How speed changes the result**: (1) The leader's own sweep is the move: in today's twelve flagged fills the +5-minute price was within 0.5c of the fill in nine cases and +1.5c to +3.7c in the other three (the largest reverted below the fill within two hours) [19], so a follower a few seconds late already buys the new price. (2) Polling `/trades` every 2 s with `filterAmount=2000` returned 1,500 large fills in 8.9 hours, about three a minute, so the alert volume is small and rate limits do not bind [18]. (3) Geopolitics and politics markets carry no taker delay (`clob.polymarket.com/markets/{conditionId}` returns `seconds_delay: 0` and `taker_base_fee: 0` on the top geopolitics markets; `clob-markets` shows no `sd` or `itode` key) [20], so the FAK matches immediately; the delay that matters is the human one between alert and decision. (4) For the quote-pull, the cost of being slow is the next informed clip, not the first; a maker with a 1-2 s cancel path loses one fill, not the position.
- **Where to run it**: anywhere with an RPC and Data API access; eu-west-1 helps the maker bot, not the detector. Not the UK (close-only) or the US on the international venue [E9]. Do not pay for co-location for this.

### Results by investment size
Assumptions: fast-follow only in fee-free geopolitics and 0.04-fee politics; clips capped at touch depth ($500-$3k) [20]; 1-3 true positives a month against 30-60 alerts; 1c spread paid; VPS $10-$65; no paid alert feed. Every PnL figure is a guess; no verified follower PnL exists.

| Capital | Workable? | How it is deployed | Gross / month | Running costs / month | Net / month | Net / capital / month | Binding constraint |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $1k | as a detector and quote-pull hook, yes; as a follower, marginal | 2-3 clips of $300-$500 held to the event | -$50 to +$60 (guess) | $10-$20 | -$70 to +$50 | -7% to +5% | fixed costs; one wrong follow is 30-50% of the bankroll |
| $5k | marginal | $500-$1.5k clips, at most 3 open | -$150 to +$200 (guess) | $20-$65 | -$215 to +$180 | -4% to +4% | alert precision; depth after the sweep [20] |
| $10k | marginal as a follower; strong as a defence for a $10k maker book | $1-3k clips, plus quote-pull on files 02 / 04 / 05 | -$300 to +$400 following; +$100 to +$500 avoided losses (derived guess) | $30-$100 | -$400 to +$850 | -4% to +9% | capital lock-up to the event; false positives |

- **Minimum sensible capital**: ~$0 for the detector and quote-pull mode (it earns by protecting other capital); ~$3k for following, so that a $1k clip is not a third of the book.
- **Capacity ceiling**: the depth informed money leaves behind, low thousands of dollars per alert and a handful of true alerts a month, so ~$10k-$20k of following capital before you are the whale you are trying to follow (guess).
