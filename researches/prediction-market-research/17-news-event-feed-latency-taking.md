# 17 - Scheduled-release and breaking-news latency taking (earnings, macro prints, agency / court decisions, election-night returns, live ceremonies)
_Band: established-emerging · Evidence grade: A (hard data for mechanism, timing and capacity: own second-by-second trade-tape reconstruction of six earnings releases, two BLS prints and one FOMC decision, re-run by the cite-check pass; official docs; live books); C for the self-reported "$32k earnings bot"; D for election-night and live-ceremony variants · Last verified: 2026-09-28_

### Scheduled-release and breaking-news latency taking
- **One-line summary**: In the 2-10 seconds after a scheduled public number prints (FOMC statement, BLS CPI or payrolls, a company's EPS), sweep the resting orders that nobody cancelled in the Polymarket market that settles on that number; the mirror image, a release-calendar cancel module, is what protects the owner's own resting quotes, which are the prey today.
- **Category**: Information edge [latency on public information; directional for seconds, then near-certain]
- **Maturity**: Established but small for scheduled macro decisions and prints: on 2026-09-16 three wallets took $99k, $19.7k and $5k of the Fed "increase 25 bps" and "no change" books 4-9 seconds after the 2:00 p.m. EDT statement [10][14]; the same two or three wallets re-appear 6-7 seconds after the 11 Sep CPI and 4 Sep payrolls prints [10]. Established but thin, probably decayed, for earnings: the one identifiable specialist wallet has $5.5k of realised PnL on 2,592 earnings-market trades since February 2026 [11]; the "$32k near risk-free" bot reported in March [1] was never tied to a wallet, so whether it is the same operator, a different one or an overstatement is unknown; the six release tapes of Aug-Sep 2026 show $2-540 of notional taken per event [10]. Emerging and unverified for unscheduled headlines (X / wires) and for election-night and ceremony feeds: no wallet, tape or PnL evidence was found beyond press anecdotes about antennas and rehearsals [20]; the one academic timing study finds X and news tied on Polymarket events [3].
- **Requires AI?**: optional, off the critical path only: an LLM can compile each market's resolution text (GAAP vs non-GAAP, diluted, rounding, "greater than" strike, SeekingAlpha fallback) into a machine rule before the release and read ambiguous releases after the parser fails. Detection, parsing and execution are deterministic; an LLM call in the loop costs more than the whole edge window.
- **How it works (simple flow)**:
  1. Build the calendar. BLS publishes dates and times a year ahead (all 08:30 ET) [13]; the FOMC calendar gives 27-28 Oct and 8-9 Dec, statements at 2:00 p.m. ET [14]; Polymarket's earnings markets state the estimated release date, the consensus strike and the resolution source in their description [9]. Map each release to live markets via Gamma tags: `earnings` (id 1013), `cpi-release` (105614), `jobs-report` (102548), `fed` (159), `economy` (100328) [9].
  2. Compile the rule per market before the print: for earnings "GAAP EPS greater than $0.45", diluted unless not published, rounded to the cent, SeekingAlpha's figure if the company omits it, "No" if nothing is published within 96 h, restatements ignored except "obvious and immediate mistakes" [9]; for CPI the bucket boundaries at the BLS one-decimal precision [9]; for the Fed the change in the upper bound of the target range, rounded up to the nearest 25 bps [9].
  3. Pre-stage execution: a snapshot of the L2 book 60 s before the print (that resting size is the whole capacity), pre-built marketable FAK orders for each outcome token up to a limit (0.99 or lower), batched up to 15 per request [E1]. None of these markets carries the taker-delay flag (`GET /clob-markets/{condition_id}` shows no `itode` key on the earnings, Fed, CPI, daily BTC and daily SPX records, and `itode: true` on hourly BTC markets) [5][8], so a marketable order matches immediately.
  4. Read the number at the source, not from a wire: bls.gov at 08:30:00 ET, federalreserve.gov at 14:00:00 ET, the company's release at its usual minute (Cintas 08:30, Costco 16:15, NVIDIA 16:20 ET in the tapes) [10][13][14]. Parse, compare, fire.
  5. Recycle. Winning tokens can be sold at 0.99 within minutes or held: the four losing Fed markets closed 2 h 3 min after the statement, the CPI buckets ~2 h after the print, earnings markets 1-2 h after the release [9].
  6. The maker mirror (the owner's actual use). Every fill above came out of somebody's resting order: NO asks at 0.956 and 0.928 in the "no change" market, YES asks at 0.888 and 0.98 in the "increase" market [10]. A calendar module that cancels all quotes in mapped markets at T-2 s and re-quotes at T+60 s removes the pick-off at the cost of about a minute of reward-band time: the two live Fed October markets each carry a $1,000/day liquidity-reward pool (200-share minimum, 2.5c band) [8], so a minute out of the band forfeits at most $0.69 of pool per market (derived), while the orders lifted in the first 10 s on 16 Sep gave up ~$5.7k on ~$139k of notional, about 4c per share [10]. For an LP bot the module pays for itself on the first print. Kalshi does this at exchange level, closing CPI markets five minutes and payrolls markets one minute before the print [15]; Polymarket leaves its markets open through the release [9].
- **What creates the edge**: A free option written by whoever leaves an order resting through a scheduled information event. IMDEA's genesis-window study puts economy markets' opening spread at 21c (monetary policy 13c, sports 3c) yet finds adverse selection low there, because "its events are largely scheduled public releases, so early takers are rarely better informed" [4]: the risk sits in the release second, and the tapes confirm it. On the Fed decision the first informed clips arrive 4 s after the statement and the book is cleaned in 10 s: 35 trades, $80.2k notional, 20 wallets and ~$2.5k of gross taker edge in the winning market, plus 28 trades, $58.7k, 16 wallets and ~$3.2k in the "no change" market [10]. On CPI the first prints arrive at +6 s, on payrolls at +7 s, on the unemployment ladder at +12 s [10]. Who is on the other side: retail limit orders and reward-band quoting bots. The two live Fed October markets each carry a $1,000/day liquidity-reward pool (200-share minimum, 2.5c band, since 17 Sep) [8], so the programme in file 01 pays makers to rest exactly the orders this strategy eats, and their books hold $116k of bids and $157k of asks within 10c of the touch in the "increase 25" market and $39k / $61k in "no change" (16:16 UTC) [12]. They keep paying because a quote that is right 99% of the time is filled only when it is wrong, and most quoting stacks have no calendar.
- **Capital required**: <$1k for earnings and BLS buckets, where clips are $50-500; $5k-$50k to take the Fed, where the prey is $5k-$50k per clip at 0.888-0.99 [10]. Lock-up is 1-2 hours to resolution [9] or seconds if you sell at 0.99. Capital is idle 29 days a month.
- **Technical requirements**: (1) Data in: calendar builder (BLS schedule pages, FOMC calendar, earnings dates from market descriptions), Gamma tag mapper, per-source parsers (BLS HTML and API, Fed press release, PR Newswire / Business Wire / GlobeNewswire and company IR pages, SeekingAlpha as the earnings fallback), L2 book snapshot 60 s before each print. (2) Decision: compiled rule per market, numeric comparison, bucket routing, a definitional guard that abstains when the release lacks the exact metric (the only case in which the March 2026 team beat the leader was "EPS ambiguity" [1][2]). (3) Execution: pre-signed FAK orders per outcome, 15-order batches, no SDK pre-trade checks (the official SDKs run "helpful, but slow network checks" [1]); standard tier 40 orders/s and 80 cancels/s [E1]. (4) Risk: max notional per event, price cap per outcome, no order after T+15 s, kill switch. (5) Test: replay the Data API tapes (1-second timestamps; `/v2/trades` takes `condition`, `start` and `end`) [10] against your own parse time. Build: 2-3 weeks for BLS + Fed + earnings on an existing execution stack; the maker-side calendar module is 2-3 days and worth more.
- **Opportunity size**: Small, spiky, dominated by eight Fed days a year. Hard numbers from the tapes [10]: Fed 16 Sep: ~$139k notional taken in the first 10 s across the two live outcome markets, gross taker edge ~$5.7k (derived: ~$2.5k in "increase 25", where the largest clips traded at 0.98-0.99, and ~$3.2k in "no change"), the top wallet alone $99k notional and ~$3.3k of edge. CPI 11 Sep: annual ladder $21.0k notional, 105 trades, 43 wallets, ~$620 of edge in 30 s; monthly ladder $1.3k and ~$80. Payrolls and unemployment 4 Sep: $5.1k notional, ~$430 of edge. Earnings Aug-Sep: NVIDIA ($85k of market volume, the largest of ~60 resolved earnings events since 26 Aug) yielded ~$1.1k notional and ~$5 of edge because YES already sat at 0.97-0.999; Costco $431 (+3 s, ~$76 edge), Cintas $537 (+2-4 s, ~$110), Paychex $337 (+29 s, ~$20), Lennar and Oracle under $30 [10]. Today's prey pool across 12 live earnings markets is $3.2k of bids and $4.0k of asks within 10c, $2.4k of it in Micron [9][12]. Derived monthly pool: ~$7-9k gross across all takers in a Fed month, $1.5-3k otherwise (one CPI and one jobs print ~$1.1k, earnings $0.1-0.5k), shared by 5-40 wallets. Direction: earnings prey is hundreds of dollars per event in the 2026 tapes and the March "$32k" claim [1] cannot be matched to a wallet; stable for the Fed while $2M of liquidity and two $1,000/day reward pools sit in that event [8][9]; every maker that adds a calendar removes prey.
- **Pros**: The outcome is public and certain when you trade, so no model risk; fees are negligible at post-release prices (0.05 x p(1-p), i.e. 5% x p of the gross edge when buying the winner at p: 0.1c per share at 0.98 against a 2c edge) [7]; no taker delay on these markets [5][8]; capital locked for hours, not weeks [9]; a free, year-long calendar [13][14]; the same code protects your maker book.
- **Cons / risks**: Execution: a winner-take-all race decided in the 0.5-6 s after the print; a team with a ~500 ms wire feed found "the orderbook is swept before we receive the wires" and "never won on pure speed against the leader" [1][2]; the first informed prints at +4-7 s in the tapes are the pack, not the leader [10]. Capacity: the prey is only resting size, typically hundreds of dollars per earnings event [10][12]. Definitional: GAAP vs non-GAAP, diluted vs basic, "greater than" at equality, the 96-hour SeekingAlpha fallback and the 45-day no-release rule can flip a "certain" trade [9]. Platform: cancel-only recovery modes and 99.13% uptime [E1] can land on a print; the earnings specialist also collects rewards and rebates, so it is picked off when it rests [11]. Regulatory: nothing in the ToS bars speed on public information [E9], but wires and X data carry redistribution terms, and anything obtained before publication is MNPI (file 30) with CFTC and DOJ reach on the offshore venue [E9]. Edge decay: the maker fix is trivial, and Kalshi has already removed the window on its side [15].
- **Expected results**: Wallet-level data [11]: `0x9727…9157`, joined 2026-07-14, 172 trades, realised PnL $44.0k, biggest win $26.7k, trades in Fed, Banxico, SNB, SARB and Brazil GDP markets, and the $99k Fed sweep at +6 s [10]; `0x2f63…6643`, joined 2025-01-12, 1,159 trades, realised PnL $77.4k, present in the Cintas (+2 s), unemployment (+18 s), payrolls (+18 s) and CPI (+7 s) tapes but also in geopolitics deadline markets, so the release share of that PnL is unknown; `0x0957…5db6`, joined 2026-02-27, 2,592 trades, all earnings markets in its last 100 trades, realised PnL $5.5k plus $1.0k of rewards and rebates, i.e. ~$800 a month; `0x5a21…9318` (76,574 trades, $1.05M realised, mostly weather and AI-release markets), first in the Fed "no change" book at +4 s. Self-reported: the observed earnings bot at "$32k in nearly risk-free profits" with a "100%" win rate [1]. Guess for a solo team that is not the leader: $0-300 a month from earnings and BLS buckets, plus $300-1,500 on a Fed day if you are inside the first six seconds with $20k+ deployed; zero in months where you are not.
- **Competitive landscape**: Concentrated at the top, wide at the tail; no on-chain study isolates release bots [E4], so the tapes are the only census. The Fed sweep was three wallets in 6 s and 20 in 10 s; CPI drew 40 wallets in 10 s and 43 in 30 s; earnings events see 1-3 takers, one of them the same specialist every time [10][11]. The March 2026 challenger (Rust, Dublin, custom client) lost the pure speed race and could not identify the leader's data path, suspecting "expensive feeds" used for other purposes [1][2]. Competitive today means source-level reads (bls.gov, federalreserve.gov, the wire itself) from a US-East reader relayed to a Dublin executor, pre-signed orders and a rule compiler that never abstains late.
- **Regulatory/ethical flag**: none for trading public releases on speed (E9 traffic light: "trading on own research, models, speed" is Allowed on both venues) [E9]. Grey: redistribution and automated-use terms of newswires and X; grey for a US-hosted reader if the trader is treated as "located in" a restricted jurisdiction [E9]. Prohibited: embargo breaches, tipped numbers, any pre-publication access (ToS §4.2 insider prongs; CFTC cases Van Dyke and Spagnuolo) [E9], covered in file 30. US persons cannot trade the international venue at all [E9].
- **How you'd validate it**: Paper mode over the October calendar (2 Oct payrolls, 14 Oct CPI, 15 Oct PPI, 27-28 Oct FOMC [13][14], ~40 earnings markets): record L2 books 60 s before and after each print with your own recorder, timestamp your parsed number, and compute the notional you would have taken at the pre-print book and the gross edge (1 - price) minus fee. Compare your parse time with the first informed print in the Data API tape [10]. Pass: your timestamp beats T+4 s on the Fed and T+6 s on BLS, and paper edge exceeds $300 for the month; fail: the book is empty at your arrival. Cost: ~20 engineering hours, a Dublin VPS and a small US-East reader, no capital at risk.

### Speed and execution sensitivity
- **Speed class**: latency-sensitive. Trade timestamps have 1-second granularity [1][10], the pack arrives 4-7 s after the print and the book is clean by +10 s [10], while the Q1 2026 earnings leader beat a 500 ms wire [1]. The race is decided in 0.3-5 s of source-to-order latency; sub-100 ms engineering buys nothing because the release itself carries about a second of jitter.

| Part of the strategy | What it depends on | If you are slower or execute worse | Realistic target for a solo team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Release detection | polling bls.gov (08:30:00 ET), federalreserve.gov (14:00:00 ET) or the company wire [13][14] | the first $5k-$50k clips print at +4 s (Fed) and +6-7 s (BLS) [10] | US-East reader polling every 50-100 ms from T-5 s |
| Parse and rule check | compiled rule per market | a definitional miss trades the wrong side; abstain instead | < 20 ms, deterministic |
| Order submission | pre-signed FAK batch, no SDK checks [1]; Dublin to eu-west-2 | every extra round trip is a place in the queue behind the leader | one batch per outcome ladder, fired once |
| Venue matching | no taker delay on these markets [5][8]; async commit pipeline since 2026-07-24 [6] | matched is not settled (`RETRYING` / `FAILED`) [E1] | hold to resolution |
| Maker mirror | cancel-all in mapped markets at T-2 s; 80 cancels/s [E1] | a stale quote loses 4-11c per share on $5k-$50k of size [10] | cancel by T-2 s, re-quote at T+60 s |

- **How speed changes the result**: (1) Fed 16 Sep, winning market: +1 s $434, +5 s $6.3k, +10 s $80.2k; the $49.5k clip at 0.98 and the $19.7k clip at 0.99 printed at +6 s and +9 s [10]. The first 5 s held ~$1.2k of gross edge (the 0.888 clip alone $630); +10 s held leftovers at 0.99. (2) BLS: nothing trades in the first 5 s, then 76 trades from 36 wallets between +6 and +10 s on the CPI annual ladder [10]; the source is the race, not the venue. (3) Earnings: the Q1 2026 bot swept books before a 500 ms wire delivered the number [1]; in Aug-Sep the first prints came at +2-4 s and took hundreds of dollars [10]. (4) Nine trading incidents in 90 days and cancel-only recovery modes [E1]: a print inside one leaves the maker side exposed and the taker side idle.
- **Where to run it**: executor in AWS eu-west-1 (Dublin), the nearest unrestricted region to the eu-west-2 CLOB [E1]; reader in us-east-1 next to bls.gov, federalreserve.gov and the wires, relaying one parsed number. Not the UK or the US for the trading leg [E9]. Co-location is not worth it: the race is won at the source.

- **Minimum sensible capital**: ~$2k; below it hosting exceeds a normal month's edge on capital that is deployed for minutes.
- **Capacity ceiling**: the resting size at the print: ~$140k notional and ~$5.7k of edge on a Fed day, ~$22k and ~$0.7k on a CPI day, hundreds of dollars per earnings event [10]; a solo operator saturates at $20k-$50k because more size only buys worse prices in the same six seconds.
