# 53 - Culture panels: critic, jury and voter twins (Rotten Tomatoes ladders, awards, Eurovision)
_Band: speculative · Evidence grade: D (no critic, jury or voter twin has a forecasting or trading record against a price anywhere; A for the market baseline measured here from Gamma, CLOB, the Data API and Kalshi's public API; B for the adjacent synthetic-respondent literature, mostly preprints; C for trader self-reports) · Last verified: 2026-09-29_

### Culture panels: critic, jury and voter twins
- **One-line summary**: Treat a mechanically aggregated culture score (the Rotten Tomatoes Tomatometer, an awards ballot, Eurovision's jury plus televote) as the sum of verdicts from an enumerable panel, model each member, convolve into a bracket or winner distribution before the information arrives, and trade the gap to the price. Unproven: no twin has a record and LLM taste is super-omnivorous; on 135 resolved Polymarket RT ladders the price beats an unconditional score prior by 0.12 Brier per rung seven days out, but by only 0.04 (interval includes zero) on ladders whose reviews had not yet landed, and each ladder's largest 6-hour move, usually the embargo, is a median 38c.
- **Category**: Speculative-novel [information edge on culture aggregates]. Widened per critic note (a) from RT ladders to culture panels; critic note (b) was not applied: synthetic audiences for pre-release demand brackets stay in file 76 and box-office counts in file 23.
- **Maturity**: Experimental and unproven. No LLM critic, jury or voter twin scored against a market price was found (nine GitHub queries, four HN queries; the one "Rotten-Tomatoes-Kalshi-Algo" repository is empty) [21]. A statistical, human version is traded on Kalshi, but its only record is one trader's claim in venue marketing: RT ladders launched in March 2024 and the trader reported being up ~$17k by May 2024 from embargo dates, reviews found before RT adds them and total-review estimates (self-reported) [14]. The adjacent evidence is weak or negative: individual twins built on 500+ answers correlate r = 0.20 with their humans [3]; 554,940 silicon respondents are "super-omnivorous" with a positive bias for liking [1]; the one movie-review persona paper (DPRF) generates review text for an assigned sentiment and never predicts a verdict [4]; aggregates fare better (90% of human test-retest on 57 purchase-intent surveys) [2]. A Eurovision voting-network model was "not much improved" over betting tables [5] [pre-2025].
- **Requires AI?**: optional. The core is statistics: per-critic fresh / rotten propensities, a roster and filing-time model, Poisson-binomial convolution, a Beta-Binomial update after the embargo. An LLM persona layer would only price films before any review exists (reading each critic's past reviews and the film's dossier); for awards an LLM could elicit guild or bloc preferences for a counting solver.
- **How it works (simple flow)**:
  1. Targets. RT: "Will <film> score at least N on the Tomatometer?" ladders, resolved on the "All Critics" score at 10:00 ET on the Monday after release; Polymarket adds strikes after reviews land (Resident Evil gained 92-98 on 16 September) [9]. Awards: Oscars category winners (side categories too), Grammys, Golden Globes, media-voted MVP awards. Contests: Eurovision winner, jury winner, televote winner [20].
  2. Roster: which Tomatometer critics review a wide release and when they file relative to the snapshot (trade press first, weekend stragglers later).
  3. Per-member propensity: P(fresh) per critic from their verdict history by genre, studio, franchise, festival reception; optionally an LLM persona per critic before the embargo.
  4. Convolve: Poisson-binomial over the expected roster at the snapshot gives the score distribution and each rung's probability; after the embargo, update with the actual early verdicts, correcting for early-review selection (outlets and critics that file first differ from the final mix).
  5. Awards: voter archetypes per guild or branch, preferential counting for Best Picture; Eurovision: 50/50 national juries plus televote, bloc and diaspora structure.
  6. Quote as maker where model and mid differ by more than half-spread plus fee (culture taker fee 0.05·p(1−p), makers pay none, 25% maker rebate [22]); pull quotes before a known embargo time.
- **What creates the edge**: Mechanism: the score is a vote count over a largely known panel with public track records, so member-level modelling decomposes an aggregate the crowd prices from hype, and the pre-embargo window and the filing-time drift to the snapshot are structural. The strongest counter-argument is in four layers. (a) The crowd knows a lot early: on 135 resolved ladders (February 2025 - September 2026) market Brier per rung is 0.154 fourteen days before the snapshot (142 rungs, 38 events, 60 Yes) and 0.146 seven days before (248, 63, 100 Yes); on the rungs with at least 15 earlier films, a leave-future-out prior from earlier final scores scores 0.266 and 0.272, worse than the market by 0.118 [0.041, 0.192] and 0.125 [0.066, 0.183] (event-clustered) [10]. Much of that is reviews already in: seven days out, 27 of the 51 compared ladders had already had their largest move (below); on the 24 that had not, the market (Brier 0.199, 27 Yes in 99 rungs) beats the prior by only 0.044 [−0.026, +0.113], and by 0.081 [−0.012, +0.180] fourteen days out (18 ladders) [10]. The pre-review window is where the price is weakest and the only opening for a panel model; the split rests on the hindsight proxy, so it is indicative. (b) The window is short and thin: ladders list a median 6.8 days before the snapshot; each ladder's largest 6-hour move (an embargo proxy chosen with hindsight: no Gamma description states an embargo time; Kalshi's own Resident Evil forecast went from 82% to 95% as reviews landed [15]) is a median 38c, ≥ 20c in 95% of ladders, a median 4.9 days before the snapshot; Brier on the same 497 rungs falls from 0.149 to 0.093 within six hours of it, and the ex-ante fixed horizons show the same step (0.146 seven days out, 0.067 three days out) [10]; across the 30 largest 2026 ladders a median 5% (mean 22%) of fill volume traded before it, $2.3k of a median $30.6k [11]. After 30-50 reviews a Beta-Binomial does the work and the twin adds nothing. (c) The LLM layer has negative evidence [1][3][4]. (d) Where money is, prices are efficient or anchored elsewhere: Kalshi's RT ladders trade ~29x Polymarket's by contracts versus dollars [13]; media-voted awards are near-certain a week out (below); Eurovision is anchored by 15-18 bookmakers [19] and a voting model did not beat them [5]; bloc voting is documented but mild and known [6][7]. What the baseline does show: a knife-edge drift late in the weekend: rungs priced 20-80c one day before the snapshot resolved Yes 11.3c more often than priced (84 rungs, 58 events), and 7-11c more often at six horizons from 48 to 6 hours; bought at the measured median live half-spread (5.75c) plus the fee, that nets +5.1c [−2.9, +14.0] a share at 24 hours, not significant even before a six-horizon Bonferroni correction (+8.3c [+0.3, +17.2] at an assumed 2.5c half-spread) [10][12]. That is a filing-time question for the statistical core, not for LLM twins.
- **Capital required**: <$1k to low thousands, depth-bound. Live RT ladders show $0-171 within 5c per side on 20-80c rungs and 2-38c spreads [12]; capital recycles weekly. Awards favourites carry ~$440-2,600 per side within 5c (other names $1-1,240) but are seasonal and locked for months [12].
- **Technical requirements**: Agent design sketch. (1) Rule compiler: snapshot time, "All Critics" versus Top Critics, added strikes, "at least" versus bracket form [9]. (2) Critic registry: roster, outlet, genre history, filing lag; the lawful source is the hard part: RT's terms bar "robots, scripts, spiders, data extractors or other automated means" and using content for "training ... any artificial intelligence tool" [18], and no licensed per-critic feed was found, so histories come from licences or critics' own publications. (3) Statistical core: hierarchical logistic P(fresh | critic, film features), filing-time model against the snapshot, Poisson-binomial convolution, post-embargo Beta-Binomial with an early-mix correction. (4) Optional persona layer: one agent per critic sees only that critic's past reviews and a dated dossier (trailer reactions, festival notices, director history); two model families; output is a single P(fresh), shrunk toward the statistical core and never used after the embargo. (5) Awards: 10-20 guild or branch archetypes elicited once per season, counted by a transparent preferential or points solver; Eurovision juries and televote per country with bloc terms from history. (6) Cost, estimated, not measured: ~300 critic personas × ~5k input tokens ≈ 1.5M input tokens a film, $0.2-4 at file 21's list prices (GPT-6 Luna to Sonnet 5), $1-20 with five samples per critic for dispersion, batch halves it; awards under $10 a season. (7) Replay harness with a book recorder. Build: 3-5 weeks solo for the statistical core on file 21's stack, plus open-ended work to assemble critic histories lawfully.
- **Opportunity size**: Small and shrinking for RT on Polymarket: 48 ladders in 2025 (median $29.8k Gamma volume, both legs, total $3.4M) and 87 in January-September 2026 (median $10.7k, total $1.8M) [9]; the four live ladders show $2.2-18.7k [12]. Kalshi's 25 RT ladders closing 27 July - 28 September 2026 traded a median 435k contracts (US persons only) [13]. Awards: Oscars 2026 Best Picture $12.9M, Best Actor $5.4M, side categories a median $122k per event; Oscars 2027 events already hold $1.2M volume and $650k liquidity [20]. Eurovision 2026 winner $193.9M (both legs), televote $8.4M, jury $4.3M; 2025 winner $105.9M [20]. Media-voted MVP and rookie awards run $2-182M [20]. A real 3c edge on the ~$1-2k a month of pre-embargo near-mid RT turnover is worth tens of dollars a month (derived).
- **Pros**: Weekly RT resolution gives fast feedback; the aggregation rule is mechanical and public; the statistical core needs no LLM and is leak-free by construction; Kalshi's larger ladders are free scoring targets.
- **Cons / risks**: Edge: (a)-(d) above. Adverse selection: resting quotes at the embargo lift are picked off by review readers within minutes (38c median move) [10]. Data: RT terms forbid the scraping and AI use this design needs [18]. Resolution: RT controls which reviews count and when; 2 of 135 ladders had a disputed rung in Gamma, while `/v2/resolutions` says `was_disputed: false` on both [9]. Leakage: every past film and ceremony sits in model training data; a 2024 Oscars-forecasting prompt study reached 100% once the ceremony was inside the training window [8], and backtests of LLM forecasters are contaminated by default [E8]. Seasonality: Oscars, Grammys and Eurovision resolve once a year each. Insiders: awards leaks (file 30); a hobby repository alleges one wallet loaded up on Burgas before the Eurovision 2027 host city was announced on 13 August (unverified) [23]. Manipulation: paying critics to lift a Tomatometer is documented (2023, headline only) [17] and a 2026 magazine piece claims to show how RT markets can be rigged (headline only) [16].
- **Expected results**: no public data, theoretical only. Adjacent: a Kalshi RT trader's self-reported ~$17k, 2024, statistical and human [14]; no scored twin. Measured market context: Oscars favourites one day out won 35 of 46 categories in 2025-2026 (headline 10 of 11, winner log loss 0.38; side categories 25 of 35, 0.65), with big pre-ceremony moves on guild wins (Anora 16.5% → 66.5%, Michael B. Jordan 7% → 54% from 30 days to 1 day out); media-voted awards (NFL and NBA MVP and rookie awards, NBA DPOY, Game of the Year 2025) had the favourite win 10 of 10 a week out at a median 94%; Eurovision favourites lost both contests (Sweden 45.5% vs Austria 21.5% in 2025; Finland 50.3% vs Bulgaria 3.6% in 2026, one day out) [20]. Guess: a statistical core adds 0 to 0.01 Brier per rung before the embargo and the persona layer adds nothing; PnL −3% to +3% of $1-2k monthly pre-embargo turnover, −$60 to +$60 a month before costs.
- **Competitive landscape**: RT prices are set by hobbyists who track embargo dates and review velocity (the Kalshi playbook [14]), Kalshi's much larger book [13], and reward quoters ($1-10 a day per rung, 30-50 share minimum [12], file 01). Awards are priced off precursor awards and pundit consensus; Eurovision off bookmakers [19]. No twin-based trader found [21]; being competitive means a lawful critic-history dataset and a pre-registered ledger, which nobody has published.
- **Regulatory/ethical flag**: grey. Trading your own model on culture markets: allowed on the international venue outside restricted jurisdictions [E9]. Data: RT's terms (Fandango, updated 2026-01-06) prohibit crawlers, data mining, commercial use and AI training on its content [18]; Polymarket's own data clause binds professional users [E9]. Never solicit, pay or pressure critics or voters, file reviews, or publish persona output as a named critic's view: that is manipulation (file 31) and impersonation. Awards and Eurovision leaks are insider information (file 30's Year in Search case). Kalshi trading needs US eligibility.
- **How you'd validate it**: Phase 0 (done here, $0): market baseline, embargo proxy, volume timing, power [10][11]. Phase 1, retrospective kill test of the statistical core, 2-4 weeks plus data acquisition, $0-300: stop if per-critic histories cannot be obtained lawfully. Universe: the 135 Polymarket ladders plus Kalshi's ladders as scoring-only targets [13]. Forecast times: listing + 6 h, embargo − 1 h (from embargo times recorded forward: the historical proxy is chosen with hindsight), embargo + 6 h, snapshot − 24 h, counted only for rungs open at that time. Six pre-registered arms: base-rate prior (leave-future-out, as here); market mid (CLOB `/prices-history`, ≤ 15-day windows); statistical core; core + LLM persona layer (post-cutoff films only); file 21's plain forecaster on the same dossier; 0.8 market / 0.2 core blend. Metrics: rung Brier and bracket log loss, event-clustered paired bootstrap; PnL at recorded books after the culture fee. Power: a forecaster that departs from the price as far as the base rate does has a per-event summed-Brier-difference SD of 0.91 at seven days, needing ~4,000 ladders for a 0.01-per-rung gain; a 0.2-weight blend has SD 0.13 and needs ~77 ladders for 0.01 and ~19 for 0.02 (80% power, two-sided 5%, derived at seven days) [10]: only blends are testable. Pass: the blend beats the mid at embargo − 1 h by ≥ 0.01 per rung with the interval above zero over ≥ 80 ladders. The late-drift hypothesis is tested here as the filing-time component, on fresh ladders only. Kill criterion, the pre-registered null: "LLM persona layer = statistical core" in bracket log loss at embargo + 6 h (drop the layer), and "core blend = market mid" at embargo − 1 h (drop the strategy). Phase 2, forward, 6 months, $50-300: frozen pipeline on every new ladder (~10 a month on Polymarket, more on Kalshi), paper-traded at recorded books with quotes pulled one hour before any announced embargo. Awards and Eurovision: forward scoring only, a guild-bloc model against the mid 30 and 7 days out on ~23 Oscars categories a year and a jury-plus-televote simulator against bookmaker-implied and market prices; two ceremonies can kill a model, never confirm one.

### Speed and execution sensitivity
- **Speed class**: execution-sensitive (fill quality, cancel timing and sizing matter; seconds to minutes). The forecast is slow, but a quote resting when the embargo lifts is picked off within minutes.

| Part of the strategy | What it depends on | If you are slower or execute worse | Realistic target for a solo team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Gamma `rotten-tomatoes`, `oscars`, `eurovision` tags; ladders list ~7 days out [9] | a day late loses a third of the pre-embargo window | poll every few hours |
| Fair value | core refresh; persona batch before the embargo | stale priors after the embargo are the loss, not latency | rerun on every new review batch |
| Entry | $0-171 within 5c per side, 2-38c spreads [12]; taker fee 1.25c at 50c [22] | crossing costs 1-19c, more than any plausible edge | maker orders, ≥ 30-50 shares for rewards [12] |
| Cancel at embargo | announced embargo time; median 38c move in 6 h [10] | resting quotes are run over by review readers | pull all quotes 1 h before; requote after 20-30 reviews |
| Late drift / settlement | filing-time model; snapshot 10:00 ET Monday [9] | none beyond fill quality | hold to resolution; weekly recycling |

- **How speed changes the result**: (1) The embargo jump dwarfs any edge: paired rung Brier 0.149 → 0.093 within six hours [10]. (2) Maker versus taker: the culture fee (1.25c at 50c) and 2-38c spreads eat a 3-5c edge unless you rest [12][22]. (3) Rate limits and uptime do not matter at a few dozen orders a week.
- **Where to run it**: anywhere outside the ~43 blocked or close-only jurisdictions (the US, UK and France among them) [E9]; LLM calls dominate latency; nothing justifies co-location.
