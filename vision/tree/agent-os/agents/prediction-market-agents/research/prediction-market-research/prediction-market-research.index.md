# prediction-market-research/

## Summary

About 40 studies of ways to make money on prediction markets, such as market making, arbitrage across venues, trading on news and sports feeds, forecasting models and copy trading, each with the evidence on whether it works, plus `_pipeline/`, the scripts and notes that produced them. The first Polymarket strategy is chosen from here.
