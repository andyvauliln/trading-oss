# 14 - Near-certain "bond" carry: pre-outcome 95-99.9c harvesting, the settlement-discount curve, cash-crunch trades and holding rewards
_Band: established-emerging · Evidence grade: A hard data (two on-chain papers, live books, on-chain holding-reward payouts, whale positions) for size and yields; C self-reported for the turnover bots · Last verified: 2026-09-28_

### Near-certain "bond" carry
- **One-line summary**: Hold near-certain outcomes (0.95-0.999) to resolution as zero-coupon bonds, priced off an explicit settlement-discount curve and a per-category flip hazard; layer Polymarket's 3.25% holding reward on eligible inventory, including risk-free YES+NO complete sets, and buy patience from cash-constrained holders into big events. It is carry with a fat left tail, not arbitrage.
- **Category**: Directional trading [carry / tail-risk underwriting; the brief's "Arbitrage" label is wrong: the break-even flip probability equals the discount you collect]
- **Maturity**: Established as a cash-management sleeve, small as a business. Hard evidence: top-100 makers of post-event "bonding" trades earn a median 6.22% APY (mean 29.08%) at ~$0.04-0.05 per trade, and the best address made $23,616 over 34,994 trades [1]; purchases at or above 0.90 return +0.83c per dollar across 588M trades, and habitual favourite buyers earn less than average [2]; holding rewards paid $11,332.78 to 48,256 wallets in the 2026-09-28 run; two of the five largest recipients hold nothing but a YES+NO complete set, and the largest holds a $5.0M set beside $2.5M of near-certain NO, all earning exactly 3.25% on the flagged part [5][6]. Decayed where it looked safest: NO rungs in the 2028 fields quote 0.999 with 846 days to go, 0.04% annualised [7]. Emerging only in the holding-reward layer (live one year, rate already cut from 4% to 3.25% [23][3]).
- **Requires AI?**: no. The screen is arithmetic (yield to end date, dispute haircut, neg-risk compression, cash-yield benchmark). Optional: an LLM rules-reader to flag markets whose wording can be litigated before you lend against them (file 64's subject); the 89.6% post-dispute agreement of web LLMs with UMA is not a pre-dispute predictor (accuracy barely above 50%) [16].
- **How it works (simple flow)**:
  1. Enumerate candidates: every market with a side quoted at 0.95 or above (or 0.05 or below, so the NO side is near certain) and at least a day to its end date. Gamma gives `endDate`, `feeType`, `negRisk`, `holdingRewardsEnabled` and liquidity [4]; the CLOB book gives the ask and depth [7].
  2. Convert price to a yield: annualised yield = (1 - ask) / ask x 365 / days to end. Compare with the venue's settlement-discount frontier (3.06% on the minimum frontier, 4.36% at the 0.1st percentile, 6.89% at the 0.5th, to end-2025 [1]) and with cash: AAVE v3 USDC 3.64% on Ethereum, 3.25% on Polygon [8]; 3-month T-bill 4.24% [9].
  3. Haircut for the tail. Buying at p breaks even at flip probability 1 - p (1% at 0.99, 5% at 0.95, derived). Observed dispute rates: NBA 0.2%, soccer 0.3%, crypto price 0.6%, tech 1.2%, politics horse-race 1.6%, politics policy 3.4%, geopolitics 4.8% [15]; only a fraction of disputes flip the obvious side, but the 2025-26 marquee disputes did (Zelensky suit, Iran ceasefire extension, MicroStrategy sale) [E3].
  4. Check the structure. In neg-risk events the NO-to-YES conversion compresses the discount to (1 - D) / (n - 1) per rung [1]: with 128 rungs the 2028 nominee NO sides sit at 0.998 / 0.999 [7]. Only binaries carry a harvestable wedge.
  5. Enter as a maker where the tick allows (0.001 above 0.96 [E1]): rest bids at 0.98-0.999, pay no fee, earn the rebate share. Taking costs feeRate x p of the gross edge: 3.9% in politics, 6.9% in crypto, 0% in geopolitics at 0.98 (derived from [12]).
  6. Recycle: once the outcome is known, exit winners at 0.99-0.999 into the post-outcome queue rather than waiting for the oracle (that window, its 2-hour challenge period and the 4-6 day dispute lock are file 13's subject [13]); clear losers at 0.001-0.005 [18].
  7. Overlay holding rewards: in flagged markets Polymarket pays 3.25% annualised on position value, sampled once an hour at random, both YES and NO valued at mid, paid daily in pUSD [3]. A split of 1 pUSD into 1 YES + 1 NO [14] is therefore reward-bearing at ~$1 with no direction: verified on-chain below.
  8. Time the crunch: when a big event drains cash, holders sell safe long-dated positions and near-certain yields rise (Neyman's 2024 reading: NO holders forwent "a relatively safe 16% annualized return" for election cash [10]). Buy into the peak of cash demand, not before it.
- **What creates the edge**: The payer is the impatient holder. Collateral on Polymarket earns nothing by default, so anyone who wants balance-sheet release sells certainty below par [1]; Gebele and Matthes show the discount co-moves with AAVE rates (r ≈ 0.365 full period, 0.302 at 90 days) and not with Treasuries, i.e. it is a crypto-native cost of capital [1]. The rest is structure: fee-free geopolitics, near-zero taker fees at the extremes (fee = C x feeRate x p x (1 - p) [12]), a Treasury-funded 3.25% on eligible inventory [3], and a 0.001 tick that stops competition at 0.999 [E1]. On the other side of your fill is either a cash-constrained holder (the good case), a habitual favourite buyer paying you to exit (neutral), or, in contested markets, someone who knows the vote better than you (the bad case: the UFO market's $615k buy at 0.998 ten hours before finalisation [E3]). The average is thin: +0.83c per dollar pooled, and the accounts that do this most earn less than the average [2].
- **Capital required**: $1k-$10k is enough to run, because there are no fixed costs and the CLOB has no minimum beyond 5 shares and $1 [E1]; but this is a yield sleeve, so it is meaningful only on a book you would otherwise hold idle ($50k+, derived). Lock-up equals the horizon: 33-120 days for the "by 31 December 2026" segment, 846 days for the 2028 fields [7], plus a dispute tail of 4 days at p99 and 14 days at p99 for geopolitics [15]. Complete sets are the exception: they merge back to pUSD at any time [14].
- **Technical requirements**: (1) Data in: Gamma scanner over the whole active book (offset pagination stops at 2,100; combine `end_date_min`, orderings and `public-search` to reach ~7,000 markets [4]); CLOB books and the market WebSocket; Data API positions and value; a Polygon RPC for holding-reward transfers from distributor `0x607C…8Bf8` [5]; a resolution-time archive (own recorder or Poly Syncer [15]). (2) Decision logic: yield-to-end screen, category dispute haircut, neg-risk compression test, cash benchmark (AAVE, T-bill), holding-reward overlay, an event calendar for cash crunches. (3) Execution: post-only resting bids at 0.98-0.999 (tick 0.001), sells at 0.99-0.999, dust clearing, split / merge through the relayer (Builder credentials, gasless) or the CTF contract with POL gas [14][E1]. (4) Risk: exposure caps per category and per news complex (the April 2026 Iran chain locked ~$489M across seven markets [E3]), a rule that freezes adds once a market is disputed (file 15), a lock-up ladder. (5) Test mode: replay of resolved markets that traded ≥ 0.95 with ≥ 1 day to go. Build: 1-2 weeks on top of an existing sniping stack; 3-4 weeks from scratch for a solo developer.
- **Opportunity size**: Three pools, all measured. (a) The settlement wedge on binaries, own snapshot of 7,013 active markets on 2026-09-28 (1,781 sides at ≥ 0.95 with ≥ $2k liquidity): median annualised yield to end date 7.08% at 45-120 days (n = 762, median ask 0.982; interquartile 1.94-12.76%), 5.12% at 14-45 days (n = 37, 92% neg-risk), 0.79% at 120-300 days (n = 617, 91% neg-risk) and 0.16% at 600+ days (n = 231, all neg-risk) [7]. That 45-120-day figure is an upper bound on the risk-free wedge because it includes genuine 5% outcomes; Gebele and Matthes' persistent-certainty frontier is 3.06-6.89% [1]. Depth at the top is deep but yieldless: 2028 NO rungs show 61k-938k shares offered at 0.999 and 130k-835k bid at 0.998 [7]. (b) Post-event bonding: the best address made $23.6k lifetime; the top 100 earn a median 6.22% APY on hour-long holds [1] (file 13's window). (c) Holding rewards: the 2026-09-28 run paid $11,332.78 to 48,256 wallets, top payout $447.64, top 10 = 23.6%, top 100 = 55.4%, median $0.004, 98% of recipients under $1 [5]; that implies ~$127M of eligible position value and a ~$4.1M/year pool (derived). At least 567 flagged markets in 29 events carry $203.6M of Gamma liquidity (the help page lists 18 events; the flag is live on more, including four "announces bankruptcy" ladders [3][4]), dominated by the 2028 winner and nominee fields (128 rungs each, $52-77M liquidity), the BTC / ETH / SOL / XRP / HYPE "hit before 2027" ladders and the 2026 midterm control markets [4] (House control: Democratic 0.92 / 0.93 on $12.5M of volume, every rung flagged [27]). Direction: the wedge is stable and cash-rate-linked; the reward layer is at Polymarket's discretion and already cut once (4% at launch on 13 markets, 2025-09-24 [23]; 3.25% since [3]).
- **Pros**: Positive expectancy backed by three independent hard-data sources [1][2][5]; no fixed costs and no speed race; fee curve near zero at the extremes and zero for makers [12]; complete sets earn 3.25% with no direction and no lock-up [3][5][6]; the frontier is observable every day, so mispriced rungs are enumerable; it recycles the same inventory a maker already holds.

Regulatory: none for buying public certainty [E9], but US persons cannot access the venue at all. Capital: a 1% discount pays for 1% of flips; category tails run 0.2-4.8% [15]; disputes cluster (Iran chain, ~$489M) [E3]; there was one refund ever, and Polymarket refused after the March 2025 Ukraine-minerals vote [E3]. - **Expected results**: Sourced: median 6.22% APY for the top-100 bonding makers, mean 29.08%, median $0.04-0.07 per trade, median holds of 0.8-2.0 h per provider, best lifetime $23,616 [1]; +0.83c per dollar on all ≥ 0.90 purchases [2]; 3.25% flat on eligible value, verified to the cent for complete sets: 3,689,354 YES + 3,689,354 NO on "Will JD Vance win the 2028 US Presidential Election?" earned $328.50 on 2026-09-28, which is 3,689,354 x 0.0325 / 365, and the Data API shows the same set accruing $21,147 of `yield_income` since 2026-07-20 [5][6][26]. - **Competitive landscape**: At the top of the book it is a queue: 0.998 / 0.999 with six- and seven-figure size on both sides of the 2028 rungs [7], and post-event bonding pays cents per trade to whoever is first [1]. ### Speed and execution sensitivity
- **Speed class**: speed-insensitive. The carry accrues over weeks to months and the frontier moves with cash rates and news, not with milliseconds [1][7]. The turnover sub-variant (sell at 0.999, rebuy at 0.998) is execution-sensitive: it is a queue-position game inside one tick, and post-event bonding pays cents per trade [1]; that window is file 13's subject.

| Part of the strategy | What it depends on | If you are slower or execute worse | Realistic target for a solo team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frontier scan | Gamma end dates, fee types, flags; CLOB asks | you miss a rung that briefly quoted 0.95 during a cash crunch; no other cost | hourly scan, daily curve |
| Entry | resting maker bid vs taking the ask | taking costs 3.9% (politics) to 6.9% (crypto) of the gross edge [12]; small sizes lose 3% to slippage and gas [20] | post-only bids, 5-500 shares, patience |
| Dispute freeze | UMA proposal and dispute events (2 h window [13]) | adding size during a dispute turns carry into a vote bet | cancel resting bids on `proposed`; act within minutes |
| Recycling | sell at 0.99-0.999 vs redeem after finality | waiting for redemption costs the 2 h window plus 182 s [17]; a dispute costs 4-14 days [15] | sell into the 0.998 bid when a better rung exists |
| Holding-reward sampling | one random sample per hour [3] | none from speed; position must be on the book at sampling time | keep sets unmerged across hours |
| Split / merge | relayer (gasless, Builder credentials) or CTF with POL gas [14][E1] | gas on tiny sets; relayer 25 req / min [E1] | batch, merge only at exit |

- **How speed changes the result**: (1) Zero for the carry: a rung at 0.982 with 90 days to go yields the same whether you buy it now or in an hour [7]. (2) Queue position, not latency, decides the top-of-book variant: at 0.999 there is no further tick [E1], so the whole 0.1% goes to whoever rested first; the 2028 rungs show hundreds of thousands of shares ahead of you [7]. (3) The only time-critical event is a dispute: the 2-hour challenge window [13] is when a near-certain price can drop from 0.99 to 0.20 (Zelensky suit: 0.19 to 0.04 to 0 over nine days [E3]); a bot that keeps buying through a proposal is the counterparty of an informed seller. (4) Venue delays and rate limits are irrelevant at this order rate; uptime matters only for exits (99.13% [E1]).
- **Where to run it**: anywhere unrestricted; a cron job is enough. Do not pay for Dublin hosting, co-location or L2 data for this file; spend instead on a resolution-time and dispute archive.

### What you are probably underestimating
1. **Complete sets earn the 3.25%, and whales already do it at scale.** Two of the five largest holding-reward recipients on 2026-09-28 hold nothing but a YES+NO set: 3,689,354 of each side of "Will JD Vance win the 2028 US Presidential Election?" ($328.50, exactly 3,689,354 x 0.0325 / 365) and 3,431,175 of each side of "2026 Balance of Power: D Senate, D House" ($308.99); the largest recipient holds 5,000,000 of each side of the Vance market ($447.64, implied eligible value $5.03M) next to $2.5M of near-certain NO on non-flagged markets [5][6]. E2 inferred set eligibility from the formula [E2]; it is now verified on-chain and in the Data API's `yield_income` [26]. A set is 3.25% at Polymarket credit risk, mergeable any hour [14], against 3.64% on AAVE and 4.24% in bills [8][9]: not alpha, but it is the yield on inventory you hold anyway for quoting, and it costs nothing to keep unmerged. If "staking" in your suite means a rate on idle collateral, this programme is the only native equivalent on Polymarket; UMA staking (file 16) is a different asset with its own price and slashing risk.
2. **The safest-looking rungs pay nothing, by construction.** Neg-risk conversion spreads one binary discount over n - 1 NO legs [1]; in the 128-rung 2028 fields the NO sides sit at 0.998 / 0.999 with 846 days to run, 0.04% annualised, and the whole 120-600-day bucket in my snapshot is 72-100% neg-risk with a median of 0.8-1.1% [7]. The harvestable wedge lives in binaries: the "by December 31, 2026" segment at a median 7.08% [7]. If your screen ranks by price it fills up with 2028 nominees and earns the cash rate minus the tail.
3. **Break-even is the discount, and the tail is category-shaped.** At 0.99 you are paid 1% to underwrite a flip whose observed dispute rate is 0.2% in the NBA and 4.8% in geopolitics [15], and geopolitics is also the fee-free category, so the fee-optimised screen points at the worst tail. Disputes are not independent: the April 2026 Iran chain locked seven markets and ~$489M for two to three rounds each [E3]. Size by category and by news complex, not by price.
4. **The average harvester underperforms the average.** Pooled, purchases at ≥ 0.90 earn +0.83c per dollar, but the top decile of habitual favourite buyers, who place 15.1% of all high-price purchases, earn less than everyone else [2]. Selection, not frequency, is the skill; a bot that buys every 0.97 is the counterparty the paper describes.
5. **Holding rewards have no $1 floor but do have a discretionary ceiling.** 47,269 of the 48,256 recipients on 2026-09-28 got under $1 and the median was $0.004 [5], so E2's "$1 minimum" for the five cash programmes [E2] does not apply here; but the rate went from 4% on 13 markets at launch to 3.25% within nine months [23][3], the help page reserves the right to cap the total and announces a migration to a new reward and oracle-resolution system "later this year" [3], and the changelog carries no holding-reward entry at all [25]. Treat the 3.25% as a promo, not a coupon.
6. **Your reconciliation sources are intermittent.** The Data API's `yield_income` field exists and, when it answers, agrees with the chain (the 3,689,354-share set has accrued $21,147 since 2026-07-20, ~$300 a day against $328.50 paid today), but `/v2/user-pnl` returned empty objects for every wallet at 15:55 UTC on 2026-09-28 and full histories nine minutes later [26]. On the RPC side, publicnode had pruned the 2026-09-27 payout logs by the next afternoon and returned HTTP 403 an hour after serving the 2026-09-28 run, which Tenderly then served only in 100-block chunks [5]. Pull the distributor transfers within 24 hours, keep two RPC providers, or pay for an archive node; otherwise you cannot audit either your own yield or a rival's.
7. **Cash crunches move the frontier against you before they move it for you.** Neyman's 2024 reading is that NO holders sold a safe 16% annualised to fund election bets and the "Jesus returns" YES went from 0.3% to 1.5% [10]; today that market's YES trades 1.6-1.7c with 95 days to go, a 6.2% annualised NO yield [11], and the midterms are 36 days out. A carry book that is fully invested when the crunch starts cannot buy the cheap rungs and is marked down with everyone else; keep the complete-set reserve liquid for exactly that week.
8. **Fees are tiny; gas, slippage and the queue are not.** At 0.98 a politics taker pays 0.08c per share on a 2c gross edge (3.9%), crypto 0.14c (6.9%), geopolitics nothing [12]; but a 0.98-entry bot kept $11 of $84 because of 2.8-3.4% slippage, $0.15 gas on $4.80 redemptions and unrealised marks [20], and at the top of the book the 0.998 / 0.999 pair leaves 0.1% per cycle to whoever rested first [7][E1]. Rest bids, batch merges, and never redeem dust.
9. **"Certain" is venue-specific, which bites only if you hedge across venues.** Polymarket US settles death or incapacity events at last traded prices (Rule 10.5) and can reverse for "obvious error"; Kalshi applied its death carve-out to Khamenei and paid pre-death prices while Polymarket's contract went through two disputes [E3]. A cross-venue hedge of a near-certain YES therefore pays differently in exactly the scenario where the international contract pays $1. On the international venue itself the one documented mass reversal of "certain" positions was inside 5-minute crypto windows, where a near-certain side was overturned one time in three during manipulated settlements before the TWAP change [22]: file 12's territory, not carry.
10. **The time-value premium is largest where a hedge exists, and that is someone else's file.** The sports maker interviewed by The Oracle sees long-dated team futures "40% below fair value compared to sharp sportsbooks" because "people on Polymarket hate holding exposure to things that happen months from now" [24] (self-reported).
